- What model actually does when it recieves a message?
1. the text is cut into tokens. cutting is dependent on the TOKENIZER, but lets take it as it cut word by word.This is called tokenization.
2. Tokens are given a number and the numbers are stored in embeddings. EMBEDDINGS are the long list of numbers. think of it as a token address on map of meaning.
CONTEXT - for a token 'order', how does claude knows that it belongs to restaurant or command or court instruction. bcs of the adjecent words/tokens, thats what is context.

TOKEN - The label
EMBEDDING - The starting meaning
CONTEXT - The final meaning

What platform controls - How a token is choosen aming the likely ones. we cannot set temperature
What we control - the request, clear instruction, good context, structured outputs and checks in the code.

The whole mechanism - 
    Tokens -> Numbers -> Context -> Next token -> Add to text --- this goes on and on --- until the answer is complete.

THREE KIND OF INFORMATION

1. Instructuion - Defines the task {<instruction> You are shop assist. You answer questions about order. Never give delivery date which you did not read from the order. </instruction>}
2. Context - Gives the fact {<context> ORD:847 Delivered on 3nd nov. Shipping policy: Fasto delivery takes max 1.5 days </context>}
3. Input - Gives the question. {<input> where is my order? </input>}
